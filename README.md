- **Finds the music on your phone by itself**: albums, artists, genres, songs and favourites, with covers. No internet needed.
- **Plays hi-res files**: FLAC up to 24-bit / 192 kHz, plus MP3, AAC, Ogg and WAV. Now Playing shows the exact source format and where the sound goes: speaker, wired headphones, Bluetooth earbuds or a USB DAC, with the DAC's name and the sample rates it supports.
- **Volume leveling** so every song plays at the same loudness, **crossfade** (0–12 s) and **gapless** albums
- **For You** mixes built from what you play and favourite, worked out on your phone
- **Themes** change the whole look, from Midnight and Aero glass to Retro vinyl, or make your own and share it
- Spotify-style gestures: swipe the mini player to skip, swipe down to close Now Playing, an "Up next" queue

## 🎚️ Sound Lab

- **10-band equalizer** that hits the exact values you set, with presets or fine-tuning in 0.1 dB steps
- **Adaptive Bass** in three styles (Clean, Warm, Subwoofer), plus **Punch** for harder kick drums and **Restore** for deeper bass on small speakers
- **Headphone correction**: pick your headphones or earbuds and they're tuned to neutral, using measurements from [AutoEq](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq)
- **Depth**: Clarity, Fullness, stereo Width and room Ambience, plus a limiter that keeps everything clean
- **Remembers settings for each device**: your earbuds, your car and the phone speaker each keep their own sound, switching automatically when you connect them
- **Works in other apps too**: your EQ, headphone correction, bass and limiter in Spotify, YouTube Music and other players (Android 9+)

## Install

1. Download the `.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Voxsama/jukio-player-releases/releases/latest) on your phone.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
3. That's it. Jukio tells you when an update is out and installs it for you.

**Prefer automatic updates from GitHub?** Add `https://github.com/Voxsama/jukio-player-releases` in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium).

## 🚗 Android Auto

Jukio works in the car (from version 1.4.0): browse your music, radio and free music on the car screen, or just say
*"Hey Google, play Get Lucky on Jukio"*. Sound Lab comes along, with its own settings for the car.

Android Auto only shows apps from the Play Store until you allow others. Do this once on your phone:

1. Open **Android Auto** settings (on most phones: Settings → Connected devices → Android Auto).
2. Scroll down and tap **Version** 10 times, then confirm to turn on developer settings.
3. Tap **⋮** (top right) → **Developer settings** → turn on **Unknown sources**.
4. Reconnect your phone to the car. Jukio is now in the app list.

## Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library, favourites and listening history stay on your phone.
Jukio only goes online to check for updates, load radio stations and Audius and Jamendo songs, download headphone
measurements you choose, and find covers for albums that have none (only the album and artist name are sent, to
MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive or iTunes; you can turn this off in Settings).
