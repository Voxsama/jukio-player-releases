## 0.1.7-alpha — 2026-10-09

### New
- A redesigned Now Playing screen with an Up next queue

- Headphone correction from AutoEq

### Better
- Punch now does real kick detection: chest thump plus beater click
- Look-ahead: kicks are detected before they play, so the limiter glides instead of snapping
- TPDF dither when effects are on, for cleaner sound

### Fixed
- Voice notes and recordings stay out of the library, and removed songs stay removed

### Changes
- Releases now publish automatically on every push
